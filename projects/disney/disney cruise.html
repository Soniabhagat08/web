About Disney Cruise Adventure

Legendary Entertainment

Experience the magic of Disney with dazzling Broadway-style shows, upbeat deck parties, movie screenings, and unforgettable meet and greets with favorite pals from Disney, Pixar and Marvel. Gear up for an epic battle of Marvel Super Heroes and Villains during this action-packed stage spectacular at the Garden Stage in Disney Imagination Garden. Prepare for jaw-dropping stunts and incredible special effects that’ll have you sitting on the edge of your seat. Witness the unbelievable powers of Spider-Man, Iron Man, Captain America, Black Widow, Thor, Black Panther and other fan favorites from the Marvel Cinematic Universe—including a hilarious appearance by the charismatic “merc with a mouth,” Deadpool himself! Cast off with Duffy and The Friend Ship, an all-new show live on the Garden Stage at Disney Imagination Garden. Join the lovable cast of Characters and their cheerful guide, Tippy Blu, as they explore the high seas on a heartwarming adventure. With captivating songs, stunning visuals and joyous moments, discover the magic of the ocean, the power of imagination and the true meaning of friendship. Meet the Characters after the show and pick up treasured keepsakes at the Duffy and Friends Shop. Extend the fun with the Duffy and Friends Discovery Quest—follow clues around the ship to find lost items and win a special prize. Hoist the sails and journey with the roguish and charming Captain Jack Sparrow and his pirate crew on a swashbuckling adventure to break a sinister curse. In this adventurous show, Captain Jack hunts for rare and dazzling treasure while facing off against the towering Siren Queen, brought to life on LED screens.

Dining with Character

Savor everything from elegant culinary journeys to made-to-order casual fare at magically themed restaurants throughout the ship. Plus, you’ll have 3 distinctly Disney dining experiences during your voyage, with a preset schedule included with your booking.Immerse yourself in the glitz and glam of Hollywood’s Golden Age at this star-studded dinner club. While dining on exquisite regional and international cuisines, be dazzled by live appearances and interactions with the most famous Disney VIPs of all—Mickey Mouse, Minnie Mouse, Donald Duck and Daisy Duck—each dressed to the nines and ready for an unforgettable evening of celebration that puts the spotlight on family, friends and sensational fun.Return to the Golden Age of cruising as you set sail on the prestigious maritime tradition of dining at the “Captain’s Table.” Located near Town Square, this elegant restaurant conjures up the bygone era of grand ocean liners with vintage maps, charts, photos and artwork amid refined art deco touches. While savoring delicious regional and international dishes, enjoy a front-row seat to musical appearances and interactions with Mickey Mouse, Minnie Mouse, Donald Duck and Daisy Duck as these illustrious world travelers share lively songs and tales of adventure.Enjoy an innovative dinner show celebrating the filmmaking legacy of Disney and Pixar Animation Studios. While dining amid film strips, clapboards, director’s chairs and display cases housing animation boards and mechanical props from favorite movies such as The Little Mermaid, Encanto and Toy Story, indulge in an artfully prepared regional and international cuisine. And watch in amazement as your own drawings magically spring to vibrant life right before your very eyes.

Action-packed kids club

Kids, tweens and teens can embark on new adventures of their own at unique, dedicated spaces overflowing with interactive fun.Kids ages 3 to 10 can let their imaginations soar as they interact with beloved Characters in themed spaces inspired by favorite Disney, Pixar and Marvel stories. Storytelling and immersive games are sure to delight tiny tots and younger kids alike. Toys, an open seating area for arts and crafts, and continuous Disney movies means there’s never a dull moment. Guided by specially trained Disney youth counselors, youngsters can set sail on imaginative fun while parents relax and enjoy the amenities on board.Tweens ages 11 to 14 have a vibrant hangout all their own. From the outside, this cool retreat is disguised as an ordinary shop on San Fransokyo Street. But inside, it is designed as if tweens had taken over and redecorated a Victorian-style café—every corner of this eclectic hangout is selfie-worthy. Guided by specially trained Disney counselors, it’s the perfect place to chill out, make friends, watch movies and play video or board games. Teens ages 14 to 17 have an ultra-exclusive retreat all their own. With a cool, retro design inspired by a vintage record store, the club is the perfect spot to watch movies, enjoy games and take the occasional selfie with some of the club’s super-fun Disney youth counselors.In this whimsical childcare center inspired by the beloved Disney attraction, little ones 6 months to 3 years of age can play and rest while being looked after by specially trained counselors and visited by special Disney friends.Designed for the littlest of cruisers, this nautically themed play area offers endless opportunities to explore new horizons. Young sailors can sit behind a ship’s wheel, slide through a pipe, climb over the bridge, play sensory-style games and hang out with Captain Mickey and Captain Minnie when they stop by!Calling all young royals ages 3 to 12! Join Disney Princesses at the Royal Society for Friendship and Tea for a magical gathering. Accompanied by your parents, enjoy delicious treats, enchanting stories and whimsical songs celebrating kindness, bravery and determination. Each young royal receives a special take-home gift designed just for kids. This experience requires separate admission and is subject to availability.

Fun & Relaxation for Adults

While the kids are off having the time of their lives in clubs created just for them, treat yourself to elevated adult experiences. Enjoy luxurious spa treatments, intimate dining, specialty shopping and captivating lounges. Poised between Disney Discovery Reef and Wayfinder Bay exists Palo Trattoria. Set within an open-air breezeway resembling a seaside village, this adult-exclusive restaurant serves up exquisite Northern Italian cuisine. Mainstays on the menu include artfully prepared antipasti, pasta and pizzas—plus, succulent seafood and steaks grilled to perfection. For more relaxed indulgences, stop by Palo Café and Taverna Portorosso. Reservations required. Additional fees apply. Nature meets luxury at this oasis of relaxation. Indulge in soothing spa and beauty treatments designed to rejuvenate the mind, body and spirit. Or unwind in the expansive Thermal Spa outfitted with walkthrough experiential showers, heated loungers, an aromatherapy steam room and dry sauna. While at sea, stay shipshape at the adjoined fitness center equipped with state-of-the-art equipment. A juice bar, wellness consultations, a meditation room and dedicated spaces for activities such as cycling and yoga are also available. Reservations required. Additional fees apply. Savor expertly crafted specialty coffees, premium teas, and a delightful selection of imaginative beverages at enchanting venues such as Spellbound, Taverna Portorosso, Tiana’s Bayou Lounge and Buccaneer Bar. Each lounge offers its own unique ambiance and playful Disney charm, making every sip an adventure unto itself.
